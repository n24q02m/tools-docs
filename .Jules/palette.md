## 2024-05-18 - Hero Action Visual Hierarchy
**Learning:** Having multiple primary buttons on a landing page hero creates visual clutter and fails to effectively guide users to the most important action. Users scan pages quickly and need clear visual cues to determine the primary path.
**Action:** When designing or updating landing pages with multiple calls-to-action, ensure only the main action uses the `primary` visual variant. Secondary paths should use `secondary` or `minimal` variants to establish a clear visual hierarchy and reduce cognitive load.
