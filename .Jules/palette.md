## 2024-10-26 - Improving Hit Areas for Overview Navigation
**Learning:** Starlight overview pages often use standard Markdown lists for navigational paths (e.g., "Where to go next"). These lists have small, text-only hit areas that are harder to click/tap.
**Action:** Use Starlight's `<CardGrid>` and `<LinkCard>` components to provide larger, more distinct hit areas for primary navigation paths on overview/landing pages. Ensure files are `.mdx` to support components.
